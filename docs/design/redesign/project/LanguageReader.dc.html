Head and foot
Controls appear in the head and the foot. Both keep their height when the controls are hidden, so the text block never moves and nothing is drawn over it.
At rest
The head shows the book and chapter. The foot shows the time left, a thin progress line and Pause. A tap on the text shows the controls, and the next tap hides them.
Under the text
Appearance docks under the text, which is laid out again in what is left.
In the foot
Speed and the sleep timer take the place of the transport, with Done. Messages such as Undo appear there too. The text does not move.
A panel that takes you elsewhere
Chapters, bookmarks and search are pages of their own.

Pages or scroll

One view
The book as its own text. The Lyrics and Word views are gone, and with them Words per minute.
Two reading modes
Pages turns page by page, with whole lines only. Scroll is one column that moves. Reading mode is the first row of Appearance; switching keeps the place and the highlight.
Scroll while following
The highlighted sentence is kept in the upper half. A touch on the text stops the following; Back to narration resumes it.

Both parts are highlighted
The sentence is one highlight that happens to sit on two pages.
When the page turns
When the narration reaches the first word on the next page. The sentence’s time is spread over its words to find that moment, so it is an estimate: about a second early or late.
Not at either end
The page does not turn at the start of the sentence, and it does not wait for the next sentence while the narrator reads words you cannot see.
Paused or paged away
A pause keeps the page. If you turned the page yourself, nothing turns until Back to narration.
