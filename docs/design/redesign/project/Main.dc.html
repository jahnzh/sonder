Where things live

Library
Every book, in either format, and your series. Filters replace the shelves list: Series, In progress, Not started, Finished, On device, Favorites.
Discover
Finds books. A result opens its Book page, and its files are found with Get.
Activity
Downloads, device copies and reading sync: Now, This device, Server. Replaces the Downloads page.
Settings
One list in three groups. Users and connections are rows here, not separate places.
Book
One page for both formats. Its Files rows replace the card menu, the release picker and the versions dialog.
Now playing
One screen with two faces, Listen and Read, and the same transport in both. Replaces three sheets and the Lyrics and Word views.
